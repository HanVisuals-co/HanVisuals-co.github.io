# Anti-Slop Audit 001 (2026-11-07)

**Project**: `hanvisuals` Official Interactive Portfolio  
**Mode**: Mode 2 (`AFTER` Audit + R-31 Decision Record)  
**Design Read**: Interactive creative-engineering studio portfolio SPA for brand marketing leads and startup founders, in a bold dark-canvas kinetic style (`#15171E` + *Sun-Drenched Bold Spectrum*), dial `ENERGY 3 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 3`.

---

## 1. R-31 Written Decision Log (One-Line Reasons)

* **Color (`#15171E` Dark Canvas, `#2D3142` Cozy Slate, 5-Color Play Prism Spectrum)**: Dark Canvas provides a cinema screening room backdrop so 9:16 video frames and the 5-color *Sun-Drenched Bold* Play Prism achieve up to 17.45:1 WCAG AAA contrast without competing background glare.
* **Layout (Asymmetric Editorial Split in Services + Dual-Ratio 9:16 & 16:10 Showcase)**: Services uses a 5/7 asymmetric editorial split to elevate 9:16 video as the flagship studio engine, while Showcase separates 9:16 vertical mobile frames from 16:10 desktop browser frames so neither format is cropped.
* **Typography (`Plus Jakarta Sans` 400-800)**: Chosen from the official `hanvisuals` brand identity (`04_typography.md`) for geometric legibility across tight `-0.03em` display headlines and compact bilingual UI labels.
* **Spacing (8pt Grid with Responsive Register)**: Section vertical rhythm scales from `py-16` on mobile to `py-28` on desktop so content groups breathe without creating tall empty voids on phone screens.
* **Cards (`Cozy Slate` Containers)**: Cards are used only where distinct media viewports (9:16 phone bezel, 16:10 browser window) or distinct capability deliverables need visual containment.
* **Logos & Icons (Official `hanvisuals` Play Prism SVG & Custom Geometric Glyphs)**: All brand marks come directly from `assets/logos/` (preserving the 5-color spectrum and pure light void with zero Cozy Slate inside the Play Prism), paired with custom 1.8px stroke geometric glyphs for Instagram, Mail, and Copy actions.
* **Glassmorphism (`backdrop-blur-md` capped at 2 elements)**: Applied only to the sticky `Navbar` and the `Showcase` project detail modal backdrop to preserve foreground legibility over scrolling content (`R-10`).
* **Motion (`cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)` + Typewriter + Pause Control)**: Expresses the `MOTION 3` kinetic studio character while providing a dedicated `Pause motion` toggle in the navbar and automatic deactivation under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` (`R-19`, `R-39`).

## 3. Daftar Temuan Audit Mode 2 (Numbered Findings List)

Kode aplikasi di `src/` telah lulus seluruh pemeriksaan **Delivery Gate** (0 pelanggaran aktif pada kode UI). Temuan di bawah ini adalah ketidaksinkronan yang masih tersisa pada dokumen spesifikasi awal (`website/DESIGN.md`, `website/PRD.md`) serta peluang penyempurnaan opsional pada komponen UI. Pilih nomor yang ingin diperbaiki jika kamu ingin saya menyelaraskannya:

1. **[HIGH] (R-02)** `website/DESIGN.md` & `website/PRD.md`: Dokumen markdown spesifikasi masih memuat karakter em dash pada judul dan contoh copy *Creator Strip*, meskipun kode `src/` sudah bersih.
2. **[MEDIUM] (R-06)** `src/components/WebCard.js`: Label `NEXT.JS APP ROUTER • TAILWIND DESIGN SYSTEM` di dalam pratinjau viewport 16:10 menggunakan huruf kapital (`uppercase`) dengan `tracking-wider`; dapat diubah menjadi *sentence case* jika ingin menghindari gaya *all-caps tracking* sepenuhnya.
3. **[LOW] (R-16)** `website/DESIGN.md`: Contoh kamus pada seksi 7 di file dokumentasi `website/DESIGN.md` masih mencantumkan kata buzzword lama (`seamless`, `Next-generation`) sebelum keputusan *Drop* R-37.
4. **[LOW] (R-05)** `website/PRD.md`: Dokumen `website/PRD.md` masih mencantumkan `Lucide React` dan `Services Bento Grid` pada deskripsi arsitektur awal, berbeda dengan implementasi final berbasis logo resmi `hanvisuals` dan layout editorial asimetris.
